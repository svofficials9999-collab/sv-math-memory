SV Math Memory: concept and data pack 14 October 2026. First playable beta. Created by D. Srinivas.

What is different AksharaNova already has maths lessons and arithmetic practice. Pop Raja is a tap/reflex game. This separate game focuses on looking, hiding, recalling and matching. No existing app was changed. Arithmetic rules and bilingual, mobile-first design principles are reused, not the existing apps' full curricula.

Five modes 1. Number trail: remember 3-7 digits in order. They appear for 4.5-5.5 seconds, or until the child taps Ready. Type the sequence with spaces. Five questions. 2. Math pairs: eight hidden cards make four equation-answer pairs. Answers are unique so there is no ambiguous match. A mismatch turns back after 900ms. 3. Think, then recall: view an equation, hide it, then answer. Addition, subtraction and multiplication according to the chosen band and level. Five questions. 4. Pattern detective: identify a constant step of 2-6 and supply the next number. Five questions. This practises pattern recognition, not memory alone. 5. Flash recall: see a 2-5 digit number for 2-3 seconds, then reproduce it. Optional. No score bonus for speed.

Suggested bands, not a syllabus map - Classes 1-2, roughly ages 6-8: operands 1-10, 1-15 or 1-20. Addition and subtraction. No negative results. - Classes 3-5, roughly ages 8-11: operands 1-30, 1-40 or 1-50. At levels 2-3, multiplication up to 8 x 9 may appear. - Classes 6-10, roughly ages 11-16: operands 1-60, 1-80 or 1-100. At levels 2-3, multiplication up to 12 x 9 may appear. Choose levels 1, 2 or 3. Older children use this as a short warm-up; it is not a complete secondary maths course. An adult can help younger children. Start easy and stop when tired.

Scoring and rewards 100 points per correct answer or pair. Maximum 500 points per five-question round, or 400 points for four pairs. Four correct earns a practice star. No penalties, speed bonus, public leaderboard or purchases. Wrong answers reveal the right answer. Best score per mode and total stars remain on this browser. These are practice rewards, not grades or certificates.

Memory mechanics and learning value Ordered recall, position recall, equation-answer association and brief visual recall are the main mechanics. Pattern mode practises recognising a rule. Arithmetic mode practises recall and calculation. Encourage the child to explain the answer on paper after the round and revisit practice another day. Games are practice opportunities, not replacements for teaching.

Better game performance does not prove better general memory, IQ or school marks. Working-memory training evidence does not support a blanket promise of such transfer. This app has not undergone child-user trials or teacher language/curriculum review.

First playable safety and features English first with a Telugu toggle, guide, Back one screen, local progress, suggestion box and Created by D. Srinivas. No account, child names, mobile numbers, analytics, ads, payments or external services. Suggestions are saved only on the device and are explicitly not sent. Clearing browser data removes progress. Storage failures are caught. Changing language during a round returns to setup rather than letting a hidden timer run.

Not delivered in this beta Offline installation, cloud sync, teacher dashboard, audio, adaptive mastery, spaced scheduling and advanced pattern families. No claims that these work yet. Real-device, screen-reader, teacher and child trials are still needed.
